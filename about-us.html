About Us

The Parent Company Behind a Family of Certified Training Brands

Industrial Certified Training, LLC is the umbrella company behind a portfolio of specialized training brands, including HAZWOPER-OSHA, ELDT Training, and OSHA certification programs. We build and oversee the standards, quality, and growth of each brand so individuals and businesses can find the right certified training to thrive in competitive industries.

Our Mission

To equip learners with the skills to advance and excel in their careers

We believe in meeting learners at their current skill level and guiding them toward greater expertise. By building and supporting a family of specialized training brands, we make practical, industry-relevant training accessible so professionals can confidently take the next step in their careers.

Our Vision

To drive growth and success through knowledge and skill development

We envision a future where every learner has access to high-quality, certified training that unlocks new opportunities and maximizes potential in the industrial workforce. Growing our family of trusted brands is how we get there.

Our Expertise

Deep industry knowledge, real-world applications

Each brand in our portfolio is led by experienced trainers and industry experts who bring years of practical experience to the table. We hold every one of them to the same standard: training that mirrors real-world scenarios, so learners are prepared for the challenges of their fields.

Our Culture

Inclusivity, growth, and a drive for excellence

At Industrial Certified Training, we cultivate an environment where curiosity is encouraged, and growth is celebrated. We value diverse perspectives, collaboration, and a passion for continuous learning.
